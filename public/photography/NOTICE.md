# Photographs and data

Source: HDR+ Burst Photography Dataset, burst 0006_20160722_115157_431,
Google Pixel, captures payload_N000.dng through payload_N003.dng.

Samuel W. Hasinoff, Dillon Sharlet, Ryan Geiss, Andrew Adams, Jonathan T.
Barron, Florian Kainz, Jiawen Chen, Marc Levoy. Burst photography for high
dynamic range and low-light imaging on mobile cameras. ACM TOG 35(6), 2016.

https://www.hdrplusdata.org/dataset.html
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

All supplied photographic derivatives and sample data are adaptations under
CC BY-SA 4.0. Changes: calibrated bilinear reconstruction, fixed display
rendering, crops, translation registration, unregistered and registered
averages, robust merge, inspection scaling, and lossless WebP conversion of
crops. The opening full-frame locator uses stride-4 point sampling and lossy
WebP quality 88. It is not a quality export or ground truth. Original DNGs
are not shipped in this blog. Direct source URLs and original attribution
are in manifest.json; derivative hashes and viewing settings are in
article-data.json. The manifest describes the original reference application;
its extra capabilities are not features of this article.
