# Darkframe Studio privacy policy

_Effective 20 November 2026_

Darkframe Studio is an iPad app for stacking and editing astrophotography captures, made by me, Asier Zapata. This policy covers the app.

## The short version

Darkframe Studio does not collect any data about you, does not track you, and does not share anything with anyone. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK that collects data. All processing happens on your iPad.

## Your images and files

- The app only opens the captures and images you choose with the iPadOS file picker or open from the Files app.
- Stacked images, edited copies and their adjustment files are written to the locations you choose. Your original files are never changed.
- Your images never leave your iPad through Darkframe Studio. Stacking, editing and noise reduction all run on the device, and the app makes no network requests.
- If your files live in iCloud Drive or another storage provider, that provider syncs them under its own privacy policy, as with any other file.

## What stays on your iPad

To work, the app keeps a small amount of information on the device only:

- A list of the images you opened recently, the collections you made and the labels you gave them, so the gallery can show and reopen them.
- App preferences, stored with the standard iPadOS settings storage.
- Progress records for a stack that is still running, so an interrupted stack can be restarted.

The app also reads file dates to show and sort your images, and the time since the iPad started to measure how long processing takes. None of this is sent anywhere. Deleting the app removes it.

## Apple and TestFlight

If you test a beta through TestFlight and choose to send feedback, screenshots or crash reports, or if you allow iPadOS to share analytics with app developers, Apple collects that information and passes it to me under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). I only use it to fix problems in the app.

## Children

Darkframe Studio collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and its effective date. If the app ever starts collecting data, this page and the App Store privacy details will say so before that version is released.
